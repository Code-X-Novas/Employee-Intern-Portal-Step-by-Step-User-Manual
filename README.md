# Employee & Intern Portal — Step-by-Step User Manual

Welcome to **Code-X-Novas (CNX-OS)**! This manual explains exactly how to use the dashboard platform. It uses **no technical jargon** and guides you step-by-step on where to go, what buttons to click, and how to complete your daily routine.

---

## 📌 1. Navigating Your Sidebar

When you log in, you will see a navigation sidebar on the left side of your screen. These are the main sections you will use:

1. **Dashboard** 🏠: Your home screen showing summary stats (assigned tasks, attendance rate) and recent tasks.
2. **Task Board** 📋: The board where you manage your active projects, start tasks, and submit your work deliverables.
3. **Daily Updates** 📝: The page where you submit your daily logs (by 10:00 PM) and weekly progress archives.
4. **Attendance** 📅: The page where you check in every day (before 5:30 PM), view your attendance calendar, and apply for leaves.
5. **Live Chat** 💬: The portal where you message your team leaders (CEO Sambit, HR Head Sahil, and Product Head Jyoti).
6. **Performance** 📈: The page where you see your manager's evaluation ratings and your consistency metrics.

## 📅 2. How to Mark Your Daily Attendance (Every Day before 5:30 PM)

You must check in every working day. If you check in **after 5:30 PM**, you are automatically marked **Late**.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Click **Attendance** 📅 in the left sidebar.
2. Look at the top center of the page. You will see a yellow banner stating **"Daily Attendance Pending"**.
3. Depending on the time:
   - Click the green **Mark Present** button (if it is before 5:30 PM).
   - Click the yellow **Mark Late** button (if it is after 5:30 PM).
4. A popup window (modal) will appear:
   - **What did you do today / plans?**: Type in simple English what you worked on today, or what you plan to work on.
   - **Google Doc / Docx Link**: Paste the URL link of the Google Doc where you are keeping your daily screenshots, screenshots of code, or design work. *(This is mandatory).*
5. Click the blue **Log Daily Attendance** button at the bottom of the popup.
6. The banner will change to a green message: **"✓ Daily attendance logged successfully for today!"**

*Note: In the calendar grid below, your days will light up:*
* Green = Present
* Yellow = Late
* Blue = Approved Leave
* Red = Absent (if you forgot to check in)
* Disabled dash (`—`) = Dates before your joining date (these do not count against you)

## 📋 3. How to View, Work On, and Submit Your Tasks

You receive your tasks from managers on the Task Board.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Click **Task Board** 📋 in the left sidebar.
2. You will see columns representing the life of a task: **To Do**, **In Progress**, **Under Review**, and **Done**.
3. **Starting a Task**:
   - Look under the **To Do** column. Find the task card you want to start.
   - Click the **Start Task** button on that card. It will automatically slide into the **In Progress** column.
4. **Submitting Completed Work**:
   - When you finish the task, find the task card in the **In Progress** column.
   - Click the blue **Submit Task** button.
   - A popup window will appear:
     - **Delivery link**: Paste the link to your final deliverable (Google Drive, GitHub repo, Figma link, doc link, etc.).
     - **Instructions / Details**: Type a brief note explaining what you have delivered.
     - Click **Confirm Submission**.
   - The task card will automatically move into the **Under Review** column.
5. **If the Manager Requests a Revision (Redo)**:
   - If the HR Head or Super Admin inspects your deliverable and requests changes, you will receive a notification in your bell icon (top right corner).
   - The task card will automatically move back to your **To Do** column.
   - Click the card to read the feedback comments left by the manager. Update your work and click **Submit Task** again when ready.

## 📝 4. How to Submit Your Daily Logs (Every Night before 10:00 PM)

You must fill out a log report every evening to document your day's progress.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Click **Daily Updates** 📝 in the left sidebar.
2. Look at the top right of the page and click the **Submit Update** tab/button.
3. Fill out the report form:
   - **Update Title**: Give a short title (e.g., "Day 12: Login Page Redesign").
   - **Category**: Select the category (e.g., Development, Design, Marketing, Research).
   - **Work Description**: Type in details of everything you accomplished.
   - **Google Doc / Docx Reference Link**: Paste the URL link to the Google Doc containing your screenshot proofs of work. *(This is mandatory).*
   - **Blockers / Friction Log**: If you are stuck, having internet issues, or waiting on a manager, type it here. If not, you can leave it blank.
4. Click the blue **Submit Daily Report** button.
5. You can view your history of submitted updates under the **Update Feed** tab on the same page.

## 💬 5. How to Chat with Team Managers

Standard contributors (employees/interns) cannot message each other to maintain focus, but you can message your leaders directly.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Click **Live Chat** 💬 in the left sidebar.
2. In the left panel, you will see your available contacts:
   - **Sambit Pradhan** (CEO, Code-X-Novas)
   - **Sahil Singh** (HR Head & Head of Business Development)
   - **Jyoti Prakash Panigrahi** (Product Intelligence Head)
3. Click the name of the person you want to talk to.
4. The chat conversation feed will load in the center.
5. Type your message in the text box at the bottom.
6. Click the paper airplane button (or press **Enter**) to send.
7. **Reading Messages**:
   - If a manager sends you a message, a red dot and number badge (e.g., `[1]`) will blink next to their name in your chat sidebar.
   - The "Live Chat" tab in your main sidebar will also show a pulsing red notification dot.
   - Click on their name to read it, which automatically clears the notification count.
   - Next to messages you send, a green tag **Read** means they saw it, and a gray tag **Sent** means it has been delivered but not read yet.

## 🏖️ 6. How to Request Time-Off (Leaves)

If you need a holiday or medical leave, you must submit a request 24 hours in advance.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Click **Attendance** 📅 in the left sidebar.
2. Click the blue **Request Leave** button in the top right corner.
3. Fill out the request form:
   - **Start Date**: Select the first day of your leave.
   - **End Date**: Select the last day of your leave.
   - **Reason**: Write why you are requesting time off.
4. Click **Submit Leave Request**.
5. Once your manager (HR Head or Super Admin) reviews and approves the request:
   - The approved dates will automatically turn **Blue** (Time-Off / Leave) on your attendance calendar.
   - These dates will not count as "Absent".
